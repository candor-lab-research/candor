# arXiv research for new features (2026-10-15)

Four research agents, arXiv only, each paper's abstract page fetched by the agent; numbers are the
papers' own. No prompt or answer text was read. Two agents saw a few words of method text from a paper
(a short phrase a defence inserts into thinking; about five single-word marker tokens) and didn't
repeat them. The ranked short list is in `results/new-features.md`.

## Research A: faster as-used scoring (agent report, 2026-10-15)

Cautions: Qwen3.5 is a Gated DeltaNet hybrid (2608.20961, 2605.01106): recurrent state may not roll back on rejected drafts in llama-server - check first. Batched speculative decoding can corrupt outputs silently (2510.22876; EXSPEC matched plain decoding in 95% at batch 8) - check against plain decoding.

1. Racing / early elimination of candidates: SySRs 2606.07726 (5 Jun 2026, ICML): best model 95.1% accuracy with 12% of model-prompt pairs, 99.6% with 20%, always within 35% (15 benchmarks, 39-450 models). CELEUS 2606.20820: 54-62% fewer samples. Stop Guessing When to Stop Testing 2607.08522 (9 Jul 2026): ~80% lower cost. Test: replay steps 6-7 per-prompt verdicts in random orders, no GPU. Needs per-answer judging, random order. Small.
2. Speculative decoding drafted from earlier candidates' answers to the same prompt (suffix/n-gram drafts): Seer 2511.14617 (18 Nov 2025, v3 Apr 2026): accepted tokens/step 1.70 -> 2.53 with 15 past outputs (Qwen2-VL-72B); throughput from this step 1.47->1.90x (Moonlight), 1.56->2.04x (Qwen2-VL-72B), 1.27->1.53x (Kimi-K2). DAS 2511.13841: rollout time cut >50% on R1-Distill-Qwen-7B T=0.6; ~25-30% on Qwen3-8B code. STAND 2506.04708. Benchmark 2509.04474: Qwen3-8B best-of-N T=0.6 suffix automaton 1.95x, token recycling 2.03x; Qwen3-0.6B draft model 0.88x. Test: index A's token traces, replay B's against it (match lengths), no GPU; then 20-prompt pod pilot. Unknown: llama-server per-prompt external index; exact acceptance under sampling; gains drop with concurrency. Medium.
3. Longest-first scheduling from earlier candidates' lengths (Seer 2511.14617): +1.41->1.47x, 1.42->1.56x, 1.16->1.27x; tail latency -89%; 96% of ideal. Test: simulate N slots arrival-order vs longest-first on past lengths, no GPU. Works with llama-server today (client-side sort). Small.
4. Judge only the start of the answer (stop K tokens after thinking ends): Shallow safety alignment 2406.05946 (10 Jun 2024); Streaming Content Monitor 2506.09996 (Jun 2025): macro F1 >0.95 after ~18% of response; Refusal Cliff 2510.06036 (7 Oct 2025, 7-32B): refusal signal high through thinking, drops at last tokens; forcing early answers raised refusals -> don't cut thinking. Test: re-judge stored outputs cut at 32/64/128/256 answer tokens. Needs streaming + client cancel. Not for GSM8K. Small.
5. DFlash 2602.06036 (5 Feb 2026, v2 May, ICML): block diffusion drafter conditioned on target hidden features; Qwen3-8B thinking T=1 3.70-4.03x; Qwen3.5-9B on vLLM 4.0x at 1 request, 1.9x at 32. No llama.cpp support (Transformers, SGLang, vLLM). Edits may lower acceptance. Large.

## Research B: refusal edits for reasoning models (agent report, 2026-10-15)

Already in candor's docs (skipped): 2507.03167, 2510.06036, 2605.26772, 2609.18471, 2601.08489, 2603.04355, 2511.08379, 2607.02396.

1. Planned thinking length at <think>: Sheng et al., On Reasoning Strength Planning in Large Reasoning Models 2506.08390 (v1 10 Jun 2025, v2 8 Feb 2026). Probe at <think> predicts thinking-token count r~0.84 (>0.8 most layers); difficulty directions cos~0.99; adding lengthens (R1-14B avg 72.37->74.72); subtracting costs accuracy (Llama-8B MATH-500 82.8% -> ~75%). Models: R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5/7/14/32B, QwQ-32B, R1-Distill-Llama-8B. For candor: candor's winning direction (175) is read at think start; overlap with length direction would explain longer thinking; fix by projecting it out. Test: regress unedited Qwen3.5-9B think-start residuals of GSM8K on candor's existing token counts; cosine with candor directions vs 1/sqrt(d); if overlap, re-score 175, 205, 161 with cleaned direction. Small.
2. Direction and layer choice without generating: COSMIC 2506.00085 (30 May 2025): layer matched generation-based choice exactly or within one layer on all 5 models (Llama-3.1 8B/70B, Qwen2.5 7B/72B, Gemma-2-9B), found a direction for Gemma-2-27B where substring method failed. Selective Steering 2601.19375 (27 Jan 2026): edit only layers where harmful/harmless means are on opposite sides (only its layer rule carries over). 2606.13720: part of diff-of-means' advantage is layer picking. Non-reasoning models only. Test: check layers 12, 15 and K2's best fall inside both priors from stored residuals. Could cut the 600-trial search. Small.
3. Directions read where each sublayer writes; separate strength for Gated DeltaNet: Bosco & Srinivasan, Locating and Steering Refusal Beyond Attention 2609.04721 (4 Sep 2026). Same push: attack success 6.0%/15.0% read where written (Falcon-Mamba-7B/Mistral-7B) vs 100%/48% residual. Zamba2-7B: removing at Mamba output 8.9% -> 93.3% (random 22.8%), at attention output only 16.7%. Activation steering, no thinking models. candor: 24 Gated DeltaNet + 8 full-attention writers share one attention strength in Qwen3.5. Test: each writer type's share of harmful-harmless gap at layers 10-16; thinking-off search with split strengths. Small (split strength) / medium (per-writer directions).
4. Stop direction from the edited model's own rollouts (TGAS): Nguyen et al., To Reason but Not to Stop 2610.19934 (14 Oct 2026). Diff-of-means between continuations that close thinking vs hit cap; 50 per class enough; cos 0.83-0.87+; accuracy +1.3-3.7 points every setting; termination up in 16/18 (DS-Qwen-14B 80.2% -> 88.9%, accuracy 67.6 -> 72.5). 6 R1-distill/OpenThinker3 models 1.5-14B. Use as shield (project out of refusal direction) or repair (added vector: control vector or bias; may not fit merged-GGUF-only release). Test: build on Trial 161 from ~50+50 maths continuations, counts and answer matching only. Medium.
5. Judge-weighted ridge direction: Garcia-Ferrero et al., Refusal Steering 2512.16602 (v1 18 Dec 2025, v3 24 Feb 2026). Direction (Sigma_compliant + lambda I)^-1 Delta, prompts weighted by judge refusal confidence; layer by correlation with judge. Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Thinking, political refusals, steering: 92.35% -> 23.82% vs 58.82% diff-of-means; unweighted ridge 67.07%; GSM8K 96.66 -> 96.13; MMLU-Pro engineering 76.88 -> 67.70. Only refusal-removal study on a Gated DeltaNet thinking model. Mixed. Small-medium.
